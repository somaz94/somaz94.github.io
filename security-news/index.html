How to read this

The deadline is the one CISA sets for US federal agencies.

CVSS rates how severe a flaw is, as scored by whoever issued the CVE (CISA's score if they gave none). A low score does not undo confirmed exploitation.

EPSS estimates how likely a flaw is to be exploited in the next 30 days. It is not shown on a CVE already confirmed as exploited, where the question is answered.
